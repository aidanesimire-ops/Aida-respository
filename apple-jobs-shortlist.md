## How to attack this list

1. **Apply first to Tier 1 (#1–#4).** These are the highest-leverage matches to your executive RE/transactions/underwriting background and pay accordingly ($170K–$263K base).
2. **Lead your materials with numbers Apple's RE&D cares about:** $2B+ cumulative project cap, $1.4B+ owner's-rep portfolio, $47M bridge / $60M refi execution, entitlement approvals, and 40–60% valuation-arbitrage underwriting. Mirror Apple's language ("capital projects," "portfolio," "transactions," "site selection," "cost drivers").
3. **Location strategy:** If relocation is open, target Bay Area RE&D (Tiers 1/2/4). If you prefer to stay East, prioritize **#17 (Data Center East Region)** and remote-friendly finance roles, and ask recruiters about hybrid/remote.
4. **Bridge credentials to highlight:** LEED AP ND (rare), FINRA Series 66/SIE, Harvard GSD Integrated Project Management, Certified ScrumMaster, OSHA 62-hr — these differentiate you for RE&D + sustainability + capital-projects roles.
