01

The thesis

Assemble the entire E Sunrise Blvd frontage at the Galleria hard corner. Each asset is priced off the rent it produces, so the in-place income covers the carry while DAWN RE controls the whole block for eventual high-density redevelopment. Structure Publix as a sale-leaseback, buy out the fractured office condominium, and hold the entitled Bayview parcel. Buy the income, control the dirt — the return is the land and its density optionality, carried by the rent.

Income producing real estate is worth the rent it throws off — here about $65M, a true blended cap of 7.0%. The block trades higher because five contiguous parcels at a hard corner are worth more assembled than apart. That spread — the premium — is what you pay for the land and the density option, and it is carried by the in-place income, not by a yield.
